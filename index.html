E-commerce redesign

Fruitly

Concept project: created to demonstrate our process. It is not a real client engagement and results are not claimed.

Business scenario

A fictional fresh-produce subscription brand with a dated store and a checkout that lost shoppers on mobile.

The challenge

Slow pages, cluttered navigation and a seven-step checkout made repeat ordering harder than it should be.

Proposed strategy

Simplify the catalogue, cut checkout to three steps and lead with fast, mobile-first pages.

Creative & technical execution

Headless storefront on Next.js, a componentised design system, optimised imagery and structured product data for search.

Final deliverables

  • UX audit
  • Design system
  • Storefront build
  • SEO and analytics setup

Images & mockups

Intended business value

Intended to lift mobile conversion and repeat orders by making the path to checkout short and fast.

2026-10-20

Why site speed is your best SEO move

Core Web Vitals measure how quickly a page loads, responds and stays stable. Search engines and visitors both notice when those numbers are poor.

Start with the basics: compress and lazy-load images, ship less JavaScript and serve pages from a CDN.

Measure before and after every change, so each optimisation earns its place.

5 AI automations every small team should ship

Small teams gain the most from automating work that is frequent, rule-based and easy to check.

Begin with lead routing and support triage, then add invoice matching, meeting summaries and weekly reporting.
